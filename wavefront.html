Wavefront: Tower Defense — Privacy Policy

gamead-supportedno accountLast updated: July 2026

Who we are

Wavefront: Tower Defense is a free tower-defense game developed by DolceFarNiente Mondo. Contact: cavusogluvolkan61@gmail.com

Summary

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, or precise location. Game progress is stored on your device (and, optionally, in an anonymous cloud slot so you don't lose it). The game is free and supported by ads.

Data stored on your device

Level progress, stars, gold, unlocked weapons and upgrades, daily-quest streaks, collection entries, audio and language preferences are saved locally on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob (banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process device information such as advertising identifiers, general location (derived from IP), and ad-interaction data, governed by Google's policies: how Google uses information and Google Privacy. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings at any time.

Leaderboards & cloud save (Unity Gaming Services)

The global leaderboard and cloud save use Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies) with an anonymous player ID — no name, email, or sign-in required. If you set an in-game nickname, it is shown on the leaderboard next to your score. Processed data: anonymous ID, chosen nickname, gameplay scores (stars, survive times), and saved progress. See the Unity Privacy Policy.

To request deletion of your leaderboard/cloud data, email us your in-game nickname and we will remove it.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is processed only by the providers above (Google AdMob, Unity Gaming Services) to operate ads, leaderboards, and cloud save. All transfers use encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Children

Wavefront is family-friendly cartoon tower defense. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad content is configured for a general audience.
