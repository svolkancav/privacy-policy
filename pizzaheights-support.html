Pizza Heights: Idle Tycoon — Support

Need help?

For any question, bug report or feedback about Pizza Heights, email us at cavusogluvolkan61@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

My progress reset after I franchised. That one is on purpose. Franchising trades your whole tower for permanent perk points, and you start again from one kitchen with those perks kept. Anything bought with real money is never reset.

My progress disappeared on its own. Progress is stored on your device only — there is no cloud save. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes it.

My income went down when I bought a table. Also expected, and the button warns you before you pay. A floor is limited by its waiters as well as its seats, and a table further from the counter lengthens every walk. Add a kitchen station — that is what adds a waiter.

Buying more couriers does nothing. There is a ceiling on how much of your production can go out for delivery. Once the fleet reaches it the buttons say so, and the way past it is the delivery branch of the upgrade tree.

Does it earn while closed? Yes, on floors that have a manager, up to a cap that a rewarded ad extends.

Ads. Pizza Heights is free and supported by ads. Every ad that grants a bonus is optional — you tap it, it never interrupts play. On iOS you may decline tracking and still play with non-personalised ads.

Offline play. The game works without an internet connection; one is only needed for ads and purchases.

Languages. Pizza Heights is currently English only.
