Wavefront: Tower Defense — Support

Need help?

For any question, bug report or feedback about Wavefront, email us at cavusogluvolkan61@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

My progress reset. Progress is stored on your device and, if available, an anonymous cloud save. Reinstalling or clearing app data can remove local progress.

Ads. Wavefront is free and supported by ads (banner, interstitial, rewarded). On iOS you may decline tracking and still play with non-personalized ads.

Leaderboard. The global leaderboard uses an anonymous ID plus the nickname you choose in-game — no account or sign-in required.

Languages. Wavefront supports 7 languages — change it from the in-game menu.

Offline play. The game works offline; an internet connection is only needed for ads and the global leaderboard.
