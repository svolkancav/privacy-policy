For any question, bug report or feedback about Bug Siege, email cavusogluvolkan61@gmail.com and we will get back to you. Telling us your device model and iOS version helps a lot.

How the game works

Only the top row fires. The five slots on the armoured wall are the ones that shoot. The ten below are staging space — turrets waiting there do nothing until you move them up.

Merging. Drag a turret onto another of the same tier to combine them into the next one. Different tiers swap places instead.

Hybrid bugs. They walk in on the ground, then take off once you have wounded them. Only tier 6 and above can hit anything airborne, so a wall of low-tier turrets will watch them fly straight past.

Common questions

My progress is gone. Progress is stored on your device only — there is no cloud save. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes it permanently, and we have no copy to restore.

Ads. Bug Siege is free and supported by ads. Rewarded videos are always optional: you choose to watch them for a bonus, and nothing is taken away if you do not.

Tracking prompt. Declining it changes nothing about the game. Every feature stays available; only the ads become non-personalized.

Offline play. The game works without an internet connection. A connection is only needed to load ads.

Restoring purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID. Reinstall the app and use the restore option to get them back.

Oyun nasıl işliyor

Sadece üst sıra ateş eder. Zırhlı duvardaki beş yuva ateş eden yuvalardır. Altındaki on yuva hazırlık alanıdır; oradaki kuleler siz yukarı taşıyana kadar hiçbir şey yapmaz.

Birleştirme. Bir kuleyi aynı seviyedeki başka bir kulenin üstüne sürükleyin, bir üst kademe olsunlar. Farklı seviyeler birleşmez, yer değiştirir.

Hibrit böcekler. Yerde yürüyerek gelir, yaralandıklarında havalanırlar. Havadaki hedefleri yalnızca 6. kademe ve üstü vurabilir.
